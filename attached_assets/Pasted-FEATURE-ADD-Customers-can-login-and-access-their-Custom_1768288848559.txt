FEATURE ADD: Customers can login and access their Customer Dashboard via the Travel Agency White-Label application.

A) WHITE-LABEL CUSTOMER AUTH (TENANT-AWARE)
Goal: A customer visiting an agency’s white-label site (e.g., /w/[agencySlug]) can:
1) Sign up / Login (OTP or magic link)
2) Be automatically associated with that tenant (agency)
3) Access their Customer Dashboard with the agency branding applied
4) View their cases and upload documents
5) Track status via reference ID OR by logging in

B) ROUTES (PUBLIC -> AUTH -> CUSTOMER)
Add these public routes under /w/[agencySlug]:
- /w/[agencySlug]/login
- /w/[agencySlug]/signup (optional; can be combined)
- /w/[agencySlug]/verify (OTP verification screen)
- /w/[agencySlug]/portal (customer landing after auth, lists their cases)
- /w/[agencySlug]/portal/case/[id]
- /w/[agencySlug]/portal/profile
- /w/[agencySlug]/portal/uploads
- /w/[agencySlug]/portal/messages
- /w/[agencySlug]/portal/downloads
Keep existing generic customer routes (/c/...) but ensure white-label routes use the same underlying pages/components with tenant theme.

2) Magic link (optional toggle per tenant)
